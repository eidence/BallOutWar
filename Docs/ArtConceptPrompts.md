# Ball Out War: 2D concept art prompts

Prompts for an image generator such as ChatGPT. All designs here are original; don't add references to existing games or characters.

## How to use these

1. Start a new chat and paste the **Style guide** first, with: *"Use this style guide for every image in this chat. Reply 'ready'."*
2. Generate the **Character lineup** (prompt 1) first. When you like it, keep using that chat, or upload the lineup image with later prompts and say *"match the style and proportions of the attached image"*.
3. Ask for changes in plain words ("make Bolt's visor bigger", "same image, warmer colours") rather than starting again.
4. Save the images you keep in `Docs/Concepts/` (for example `bolt-turnaround-v2.png`) so they can be used for modelling.

## Style guide (paste first)

> **Game:** Ball Out War, a 4-player arena party game for Android phones. Each player drives a round bumper-car along their own goal line and blocks or blasts balls away from their goal.
>
> **Art style:** stylised, toy-like 3D cartoon rendered as clean concept art. Chunky rounded shapes, big heads and small bodies (about 1:1), bold saturated colours, simple readable silhouettes that work at small phone sizes, soft studio lighting with a gentle rim light, minimal texture detail. Friendly and energetic, suitable for all ages.
>
> **World:** a neon sci-fi arena. Dark gunmetal and navy surfaces, glossy brushed-metal floor, glowing cyan light strips, and each player's goal lit in their seat colour.
>
> **Seat colours:** blue #3399FF, red #FF4D4D, yellow #FFC726, green #4DE673.
>
> **Characters:**
> - **Bolt:** a small scrap-yard robot. Boxy steel head with a glowing cyan visor, round bolt "ears", short antenna with a red bulb, chest panel.
> - **Mitzi:** a street-smart orange cat. Cream muzzle and belly, pink inner ears, green eyes, confident smirk, curled tail.
> - **Hopper:** a bouncy pond frog. Bright green, pale yellow belly, big round eye bulbs on top of the head, pink cheek blush, wide grin.
> - **Pip:** a speedy little bird. Sky-blue feathers, white belly, orange diamond beak, three-feather dark-blue crest, small wings.
